## [0.1.46] - 2026-11-22

### Blender
- **Publish Anim**: the camera USD holds the camera again. Blender 5.2.2 wrote the camera as an empty transform (sh012a v007 had no camera in Houdini); the camera keeps its usual prim path, and a camera export without a camera now fails the publish instead of passing silently.

### Houdini
- **Mono Solaris desktop**: Houdini's Solaris layout with the MonoFX Loaders manager docked on the left (Desktop menu > Mono_Solaris); the floating manager window still opens as before.
- **MonoFX Loader manager**: the inspector's Details section moved to the bottom, under the prim tree, so the prims start right under the loader's name.
- **MonoFX Loader manager**: groom curves get per-shot render settings. In the inspector, a curves row's **Scheme** says the strands' shape (Linear, B-spline, Catmull-Rom) and a new **Width** column shows its width scale; both show the shot's own values in blue. Right-click curves rows (or Groom, or a selection) for **Curve shape** (B-spline and Catmull-Rom keep the roots pinned to the scalp; viewport and render alike), **Width (Karma)** (x0.5 to x3) and **Render as (Karma CPU)** (rounded, flat or oriented ribbons). Each pick is one undo step, leaves the groom cache as it is, is kept by Bake and copied by "Same overrides on the other loaders", and **Remove the shot's overrides** takes it off. Loaders from before this need the new HDA (run the Setup).
- **MonoFX Loader**: a shot's groom now renders with the lookdev's curve shape (type, basis and pinned roots). The shot's groom cache used to hide it, so char_Helen rendered as straight segments in sh025 although her lookdev makes her hair a smooth B-spline pinned to the scalp. A cache with curves under 4 control points keeps its own shape.
- **MonoFX Loader**: a groom cache's bounds now follow the hair. The lookdev's rest-pose bounds were kept (char_Helen's sat at the origin while her hair moved with her body), so framing the groom or a camera on it pointed at empty space.
- **MonoFX Loader manager**: drawn at Houdini's own text size by default (it read about 1.2x bigger); right-click the list's header for the panel size (Like Houdini, Medium, Large), remembered.
- **MonoFX Loader manager, Add assets**: picks stay when a type chip or the search hides them (the line under the cards says how many are hidden), and are cleared after Add or a drop, so a second Add no longer makes them again. Shift+click picks a range, **Clear** unpicks all, double-click adds that asset at once. Dragging a card that is not picked carries that card alone; dragging a picked card carries every pick. The cards say "in the scene" as soon as an asset is added.
- **MonoFX Loader manager**: **+** opens Add assets at once; loading a shot's anim version moved to the shot's menu (sh025 ▾). The row's right-click menu is shorter (what changes the loader, where to find it, then Bake), with an icon on every item; the version menu is compact. The top bar's icons stay together when the window is narrow.
- **MonoFX Loader manager**: click the dropdown arrow in a version cell (Anim / Model, Lookdev, Groom, Cloth) to pick another version from what is on disk, newest first, with its date and who saved its work file; the pick is one undo step, and with several loaders selected it sets them all. The first column is now called **Anim / Model** (it said Geo).
- **MonoFX Loader manager**: drag assets from **Add assets** onto a Network Editor (a loader lands under the cursor), a Scene Viewer showing /stage, or the loaders list.
- **MonoFX Loader manager**: the list stays a table at any width and scrolls sideways; right-click its header to show or hide columns. The column menus stay open while you tick columns. In the inspector, a narrower PRIM column gives its room to the last column.
- **MonoFX Loader manager**: a camera's **Aperture** is a menu (the publish's, a set width, a set width and ratio); on the publish's aperture it shows the camera's real aperture and focal length instead of empty fields.
- **MonoFX Loader manager**: the viewport selection is read from Houdini's idle loop, never in the middle of a cook (a read there can fail the cook with "Infinite recursion in evaluation"), and selecting prims from the tree adds no undo step.
- **MonoFX Loader manager**: what is selected in the viewport and in the inspector's prim tree now follow each other. A prim picked in the viewport selects its row in the tree, and its loader in the list when another one was selected. Rows picked in the tree select their prims in the viewport. It follows what the viewport draws (the node with the display flag, not the selected node) and finds loaders the shot layout moved, e.g. /World/anim/actors/char_Helen.
- **MonoFX Loader manager**: Shift+click a group's arrow in the inspector tree folds or opens it and everything under it; Ctrl+click folds or opens the whole tree.
- **MonoFX Loader manager**: the hover in the inspector tree covers the whole row; it stopped short of the name, leaving the indentation and arrow dark. A hovered or selected row is one even band over its separators, and the show / viewport / render icons are centred in their row.
